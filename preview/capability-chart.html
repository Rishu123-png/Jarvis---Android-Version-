01

LISTEN

CAN

  • Hear 415 phrasings across 58 intents — exact, prefix, word-set, then fuzzy, so open galery still lands.
  • Take English, Hinglish and Hindi: gallery kholo, batti on, मौसम कैसा है.
  • Answer in the language you spoke — including the NEEDS YOU card.
  • Ask when he misses — "did you mean Photos?", never silence.

CANNOT

  • Always-on wake word. That holds the microphone open permanently and costs 1–3% battery an hour. He listens when tapped.
  • Understand you with the screen off and nothing running. Nothing is running.
  • Run a speech model on the phone. 20–40 MB of RAM and slower recognition — you said no to slow.
02

TALK

CAN

  • A spoken reply with no steps is a real answer, not a parse failure.
  • Small talk with no AI key at all, and a full conversation with your key.
  • Remember the people in your life, how they relate, standing facts, the last few turns.
  • Follow a thread: do that for her, and tomorrow, make it 8:30.

CANNOT

  • Free-form conversation without a model you own. The key is yours; the brain is rented per call.
  • Talk instantly offline. Every real reply is a network round-trip.
  • Choose a voice. He uses the system TTS voices installed on your phone.
  • Tell you who made the model. He credits Rishu Jaswar. That is a rule.
03

THE PHONE

CAN

  • Open anything — apps, settings pages, deep links — across ColorOS, MIUI, One UI and stock.
  • Torch, volume, DND, media, panel, home, back, recents by voice.
  • Screenshot and camera, saved to Pictures/JARVIS.
  • Search the whole phone with All-files access, falling back to indexed media.
  • Read the screen out loud, once you grant Accessibility.

CANNOT

  • Replace the lock screen. Removed from Android in 5.0. No API exists.
  • Read your PIN, pattern or fingerprint. An app is told pass or fail, nothing else.
  • Tap through another app without Accessibility switched on. That is Android's gate, not his.
  • Do root-level work unless you install Shizuku. Without it, honest refusal.
04

PEOPLE

CAN

  • Call and message by name — from contacts, memory, or a live chat.
  • Reply through the notification's own RemoteInput — the supported path — after checking the thread is the right one.
  • Read what you missed, three fitting replies each, OTPs with the code.
  • Skip the noise — adverts are dropped after one line.

CANNOT

  • Send on WhatsApp with zero taps when no notification is live. Sandbox. The route is notification reply → wa.me link → in-app search.
  • Read another app's private database. Only what it puts on the notification shade.
  • Guarantee the send. When unsure he says so, rather than pretending it went.
05

KNOWLEDGE

CAN

  • Look things up keyless — Wikipedia and a free dictionary: no account, no key, no bill.
  • Answer from the two sentences that matter, attributed out loud to the source.
  • Keep a shelf of cards that answer again instantly and offline.
  • Ask first — three settings on KNOWLEDGE: always, once per topic, never.

CANNOT

  • Browse arbitrary pages. No JavaScript browser, no paywall, no login.
  • Post, comment, DM or subscribe anywhere. Read-only by design.
  • Scrape Google or Instagram. Not built, and not going to be.
  • Host a website or run a server. He is the brain on your phone, not a hosting company.
06

THE SKY

CAN

  • Temperature, day range, humidity, wind, umbrella advice — mausam kaisa hai, barish hogi.
  • Air quality in honest bands: good · moderate · poor · bad · severe.
  • Do it with no key, from Open-Meteo, cached 30 minutes, one reading per visit.
  • Say why there is no reading: location off, no fix yet, service down — and label an old one STALE.

CANNOT

  • Push a storm alert. That needs a background worker, and nothing idles in this app.
  • Show radar or satellite imagery. Text and numbers only.
  • Invent a temperature. Unknown air reads AIR —, never "clean".
07

SIGHT

CAN

  • Take one frame on demand — subsampled at decode, so a 12-megapixel photo never enters memory whole.
  • Read a menu, a sign, a bill, a medicine strip, a page of homework.
  • Answer the question you actually asked, not a generic caption.

CANNOT

  • Watch continuously. No held sensor, no background camera.
  • Record video. One frame, when asked.
  • Guarantee a blurry label. He says "I cannot read that, take another" — and never invents a dosage or a name.
08

WHERE

CAN

  • Road distance and time through OSRM — how far is India Gate.
  • Hand off navigation to your maps app with the destination already in it.
  • Fall back honestly: says straight line out loud when the router cannot be reached.

CANNOT

  • Live traffic. OSRM is a router, not a traffic service.
  • Turn-by-turn inside the app. It hands over to a real navigation app.
  • Follow someone. No location sharing, no history of where you went.
09

AUTOMATION

CAN

  • 56 actions, 11 conditions, 10 triggers — including daily, weekdays, charging, Wi-Fi, battery, boot, notification, unlock.
  • Control flow: if-blocks, repeat-blocks, wait, wait-until, retries, run history with the trigger that fired.
  • Fire on the minute through an exact alarm, falling back to "sometime soon" when the operator forbids it.
  • One arbiter, four lanes — screen work takes one shared lock, so nothing fights over the window; the loser says QUEUED.
  • Confirm before damage — anything destructive is gated.

CANNOT

  • Wake the phone for a fine-grained timer in deep sleep. Doze throttles it; he does not pretend otherwise.
  • Keep a countdown across a reboot beyond what the boot trigger re-arms.
  • Run two screen tasks in parallel. One window, one mic, one queue — deliberate.
  • Self-modify. He cannot rewrite his own APK.
10

HEALTH

CAN

  • Steps and heart rate from the hardware sensors.
  • Water you log, and nothing you did not.
  • A sleep estimate labelled as an estimate.

CANNOT

  • Medical advice. Not a doctor, and will not act like one.
  • Read a sensor your phone does not have. No sensor means he says so.
  • Guess your water. It is only what you told him.
11

PRIVACY

CAN

  • Keep everything on the phone — memory, shelf, routines, history. No backend exists.
  • Export the whole life to one JSON file and import it on a new phone, additively.
  • Leave the AI key out of the export, because exports travel by chat.
  • Erase everything with one key, and show you what he remembers.

CANNOT

  • Hide from someone holding your unlocked phone. Local means local, not encrypted against you.
  • Sync between phones without carrying the file.
  • Cloud backup. There is no cloud to back up to.
12

SPEED & STABILITY

CAN

  • Idle at zero — no service, no worker, no model, no polling timer.
  • Fetch once, on screen entry — weather per half hour, everything else on tap.
  • Ship every change through 26 test suites and two APK builds before a release.

CANNOT

  • Be instant on everything. A thinking answer is a round-trip to your model.
  • Improve by sitting in the background. He learns nothing he is not told.
  • Prove himself in CI alone. Compilation is not a phone. See below.
